The short version

Fulcrum is made by Gaurav Patel. We do not run any servers and do not collect, receive, sell or store your personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your progress and settings are saved in a small file on your own device so the game remembers you. We never receive it. It is removed if you delete the game.

Purchases

The optional full game unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or Apple account details. The game only records on your device that the unlock is owned, and can restore it through Apple with Settings, Restore purchase.

Tilt control

If you choose tilt control, the game reads your iPhone's motion sensors to steer the ball. That reading is used only while you play and never leaves your device.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
