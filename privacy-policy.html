Fulcrum Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Fulcrum is a ball-rolling game for iPhone. It is made by Gaurav Patel. This page explains what happens to your information when you play.

What we collect

Nothing. Fulcrum does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress and settings are saved only on your device. They are removed if you delete the app.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those purchases.

Tilt control

If you choose tilt control, the game reads your iPhone's motion sensors to steer the ball. That reading is used only while you play and never leaves your device.

Children

Fulcrum is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
