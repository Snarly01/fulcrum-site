Fulcrum Support

Need help? Send us a message here and we'll reply as soon as we can.

I bought the full game but it's locked

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchase, using the same Apple ID you bought it with.

The controls feel wrong

Settings has three control styles: joystick, arrows and tilt. If you use tilt, tap Recalibrate tilt while holding your iPhone comfortably.

The game is too hard

Turn on Relaxed mode in Settings for unlimited lives.

My iPhone gets warm

Lower the Graphics setting in Settings.
