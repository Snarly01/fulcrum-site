I bought the full game but it's locked

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchase, using the same Apple ID you bought it with.

The controls feel wrong

Settings has three control styles: joystick, arrows and tilt. If you use tilt, tap Recalibrate tilt while holding your iPhone comfortably.

The game is too hard

Turn on Relaxed mode in Settings for unlimited lives.

My iPhone gets warm

Lower the Graphics setting in Settings.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. It is removed if you delete the game. A purchase can be brought back with Restore purchase.

Does it cost anything?

The game is free to download and the first two worlds are free. One optional payment unlocks all the other worlds and premium balls. There are no ads and no subscription.

Something is broken

Open an issue at github.com/Snarly01/fulcrum-site/issues with your iPhone model and iOS version and what happened, and we will look into it.
